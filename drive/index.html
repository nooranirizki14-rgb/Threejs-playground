❓ HOW TO CHILL

⛽ Fuel: watch the gauge. Pull into an SPBU, park by a pump, get out (E), walk to the rear of the car, hold E to refuel.

🔧 Breakdown: redlining, crashes & dirt roads wear the engine. Repair with a kit at the hood, get the garage to fix it, push the car to a station (hold E behind it), or call a tow (T).

🛢️ Jerry can (1): +15L anywhere. 🍩 Snack (3): food. ☕ Coffee (4): energy.

💡 H high-beam · 📯 K horn · ↩️ Z/X signals · 👀 hold C peek back.

🧍 Hitchhikers wait by the road — stop and pick them up for tips.

🛞 Visit a Bengkel (garage) for servicing and car upgrades.

😴 Tired? Sleep at a motel (also saves), nap at a diner rest stop.

🚗 Auto-drive (G): cruises the lane at 80. Braking/steering takes over.

📷 Photo mode (P): hide the HUD and snap a shot. ⚙️ Settings has volume/quality/mouse.

💰 You earn vlog money per km. Prices in Rupiah. Drive safe — it's wet out there.
